# TAURARO: Universal Space Exploration Health Monitoring & Autonomous Triage Platform
**2026 NASA International Space Apps Challenge**
**Challenge:** Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions
**Official Data Alignment:** NASA Human Research Program (HRP Standard Measures) & NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)

## Core Mission & Problem Statement
As space exploration expands across Low Earth Orbit (ISS/Commercial LEO), the Lunar Surface (Artemis), Deep-Space Transit, and Mars, missions are becoming longer and independent from Earth. Astronauts are not medical doctors. TAURARO empowers crew members to:
1. Know WHEN and HOW to take health measurements accurately in different gravity fields.
2. Understand WHY each measurement matters and visualize organ-system status on an Interactive Anatomical Body Twin without medical jargon.
3. Combine physiological metrics with Space Nutrition, Hydration, Vitamin D, and ARED resistive exercise to prevent compounding risks (such as microgravity bone mineral loss + dehydration triggering mission-ending kidney stones).
4. Execute step-by-step non-doctor Emergency Response & Microgravity CPR when a crewmate suddenly collapses (e.g., acute chest pain halfway to Mars during an 18-minute communication blackout).
5. Adapt seamlessly across 4 Exploration Environments (`ORBITAL_STATION`, `LUNAR_SURFACE`, `DEEP_SPACE_TRANSIT`, and `EARTH_ANALOG_CLINIC` for rural low-resource telemedicine).
